## 2. Key Research Findings

### 2.1 Loneliness vs Social Isolation (NIH NIA)

> **Loneliness** = distressing feeling of being alone. **Social isolation** = lack of social contacts.
> You can live alone and not feel lonely. You can feel lonely while with others.

**Risk factors for Charlzs:**
- Loss of mobility
- Inability to leave home without help
- Living alone
- Lack of transportation
- Death of spouse
- Lack of social support

**Health impacts:**
- Heart disease, depression, cognitive decline, dementia
- Weakened immune function
- Chronic inflammation
- Earlier death

**NIA recommendations:**
- Find enjoyable activity / restart old hobby / learn something new
- Schedule daily contact (email, call, text)
- Use video chat if possible
- Consider pet
- Stay physically active (adapted)
- Connect with neighbors
- Faith-based organizations
- Join a cause / volunteer

### 2.2 Purpose in Life — Systematic Review (PMC, 44 studies)

**6 concepts of PIL in older adults:**
1. Health and well-being (106 votes)
2. Meaningful aims and goals (48 votes)
3. Inner strength (29 votes)
4. Social relationships (27 votes)
5. Mattering to others (6 votes)
6. Spirituality and religiousness (4 votes)

**Critical finding:** Employment provides social integration and identity. Retirement changes that structure → existential crisis. PIL drops with old age.

**Protective effects of PIL:**
- Protects against cognitive decline, Alzheimer's, disability
- 17% reduced risk of all-cause mortality
- Lower depression, anxiety
- Higher life satisfaction

**Determinants of PIL:**
- Social integration (belonging, being wanted, respected, loved)
- Inner strength (resilience, sense of coherence, self-transcendence)
- Health status
- Mattering to others

### 2.3 Frankl's 9 Paths to Purpose (Psychology Today)

| # | Path | Application for Charlzs |
|---|------|------------------------|
| 1 | Work mission | Not applicable (retired) |
| 2 | Love and friendship | Critical — but limited by isolation |
| 3 | Compassion for others | **Key path** — even brief connections matter |
| 4 | Small joys and pleasures | Ikigai — joy in small things |
| 5 | Staying strong and healthy | Independence as accomplishment |
| 6 | Creative projects and play | Hobbies that touch deeply |
| 7 | Contributing to world | Letters, advice, being a role model |
| 8 | Leaving a legacy | Not assets — wisdom, stories, witness |
| 9 | **Bearing suffering with grace** | **Most relevant** — dignity itself becomes purpose |

> Frankl: «The sort of person the prisoner became was the result of an inner decision, and not the result of camp influences alone.»

## 3. Customer Journey — Charls

### Stage 0: Existential Void (Pre-contact)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Emotional State** | Emotional emptiness, not depression. «Neither bad nor good — just empty». Apathy, emotional numbness |
| **Pain Point** | No reason to get out of bed. No «for the sake of what». Time flows but is not filled. «Days are like water drops» |
| **Skill Intervention** | Do NOT offer goals. Do NOT ask «what are your dreams?». Instead — **presence**: «I am here. And I am interested in how you are today» |

**Key insight:** Charls does not want to «plan life». He wants to feel that he is *still alive*. First step is not a goal — **quality of presence**.

### Stage 1: Micro-Connection (First Contact)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Emotional State** | Surprise that someone asks not «how is your health?» but «what did you see in the window today?». Mild wariness: «Why this conversation?» |
| **Pain Point** | All conversations reduce to illness/weather/prices. «How much longer?» |
| **Skill Intervention** | **«Sensory anchor»** — questions about specific, physical, here-and-now: «How does your tea smell today?» / «What did you see in the window this morning?» / «What sound was outside today?» |

**Why this works:** With chronic isolation, brain switches to survival mode. Sensory questions return to body and present moment — grounding technique adapted for elderly.

### Stage 2: Temporal Anchors (Restoring Structure)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Emotional State** | «If tomorrow is like today — why tomorrow?» Fear of endless repetition |
| **Pain Point** | No temporal markers. Doesn't know what day it is. Weeks blur together |
| **Skill Intervention** | **«Ritual anchors»** — not schedule, but rituals: «Tea at 3 PM with window watching» / «Friday — day to call one place» / «First of month — check the plant on the windowsill» |

**Key insight:** Do NOT «plan the day» (requires executive function, depleted in Charls). Instead: «find 1–2 anchors that create rhythm».

### Stage 3: Mattering (Sense of Significance)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Emotional State** | «I am not needed by anyone. No one waits for me». Deep sense of social uselessness |
| **Pain Point** | No role. Not father, not grandfather, not worker, not neighbor, not volunteer. «I am nobody» |
| **Skill Intervention** | **«Micro-contribution»** — find what CHARLS can give the world from his apartment: «Tell me about your city in the 60s — I will remember» / «You have a plant? Let's give it a name and a watering schedule together» / «Can I tell you a story, and you tell me what you think?» |

**Why this works:** Research shows «mattering to others» (feeling significant to others) is one of the strongest predictors of Purpose in Life in elderly. Even microscopic contribution — it is real.

### Stage 4: Legacy & Meaning (Meaning Through Memory)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Emotional State** | «Everything I did — gone. No one to pass to». Bitterness of lost connection with past |
| **Pain Point** | Fear that after death nothing remains. No heirs, no one to tell |
| **Skill Intervention** | **«Living memory»** — not «write memoirs» (too big), but «tell me one story. I will remember» / «What advice would you give a 20-year-old?» / «What did you know at 40 that you didn't know at 20?» |

**Key insight:** For Charls, legacy is not money or property. It is **witness**. That someone HEARD his story. The skill becomes a «keeper of memory».

### Stage 5: Suffering as Purpose (Accepting Limitations)

| Dimension | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Emotional State** | «I can't do anything anymore. Why live?» Despair from bodily limitations |
| **Pain Point** | Every morning — reminder of loss: can't go out, can't cook complex food, can't hear well |
| **Skill Intervention** | **«Dignity in limitations»** (Frankl, Path #9): «You didn't choose these limitations. But you choose how to be with them. Today — how do you want to live this day?» / «There are things you don't control. What do you control?» |

**Why this works:** Frankl observed that those who found meaning in suffering (not justifying it, but accepting the challenge) survived. For Charls this could be: «I can be an example of dignity for someone» or simply «I choose how to meet this day».

## 4. Hooks for Charls in SKILL.md

### Phase 0/1: Calibration (Persona Detection)

**Detection triggers:**
- User says: «I am a pensioner / old / can't do anything»
- Mentions chronic illness + limited mobility
- Mentions «no children / no one to call / no one"
- Calendar empty or contains only medical entries

**Calibration Questions:**
```
«Хотите — подберём подход, который учитывает ваш ритм и возможности?»
«Как вы обычно проводите день? Мне важно понять, что для вас комфортно»
«Есть ли что-то, что вы делаете каждый день — даже маленькое?»
```

**If Charls-type detected:**
- Load `references/mode_elder.md`
- Do NOT load `references/mode_adhd.md` — different audience
- Do NOT load standard `references/goal_architecture.md` — too ambitious
- Adapt `references/weekly_review.md` — micro-check-in format

### Phase 2: Deep Why (Adaptation)

**Standard Deep Why:** «Why is this goal important? Where will it lead?»
**For Charls:** Not goal, but **«What gives meaning?»**

```
«Не нужно строить планы. Давайте найдём то, ради чего сегодня стоит проснуться»
«Когда вы думаете о своей жизни — что было по-настоящему?»
«Если бы кто-то спросил вас: 'Чему вы научились за жизнь?' — что бы вы сказали?»
```

### Phase 3: Weekly Review → «Micro-Check-In»

**Standard:** Wheel of Life (11 domains) + Habit Tracker
**For Charls:** 3 questions, 5 minutes

```
«На этой неделе:
1. Что было хорошего? (даже крошечное)
2. Что было трудного? (можно просто назвать)
3. Что вы хотели бы на следующей неделе — если захотите?»
```

**Why:** Full Wheel of Life (Career, Finance, Romance) — provocation for Charls, who has none. Needs adapted Wheel without «failing» domains.

### Phase 5: Calendar (Daily Anchors)

**Standard:** Energy scheduling, workload warning, smart proposals
**For Charls:** «Day anchors» — not tasks, but rituals

```
«Давайте найдём 2–3 якоря:
- Утро: что поможет вам почувствовать, что день начался?
- День: есть ли звонок/контакт/активность, которую можно запланировать?
- Вечер: что поможет закрыть день с ощущением 'я прожил его'?»
```

**Example anchors for Charls:**
- 10:00 — tea + «tell AI one thing»
- 15:00 — water plant + 5 minutes at window
- 20:00 — watch show + «share a thought»
- Once a week — «story from life» (skill records)

## 5. Proposed Reference File: `references/mode_elder.md`

**Structure (≤120 lines):**

1. **Framing** — not «helping old person be productive», but «helping feel alive»
2. **Solo Aging** — normalization: «No children is not failure, just a different path»
3. **Micro-anchors** — rituals instead of schedules
4. **Mattering** — ways to feel significant from home
5. **Legacy through memory** — passing experience, not accumulating
6. **Suffering with dignity** — Frankl frame for bodily limitations
7. **Anti-patterns** — don't offer «5-year plans», don't ask «what are your goals?", don't impose «productivity»

## 6. Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Patronization** («poor old man») | Tone — not pity, but **respect for experience**. «You have lived more than many have read» |
| **Medicalization** | Don't diagnose, don't advise medicine. Focus on **quality of day**, not health |
| **Technology barrier** | Don't suggest «download app». Everything through conversation. If phone is button-only — ok, work with what exists |
| **Deep depression** | If user mentions suicide — explicit disclaimer: «I am not a doctor. You need to talk to a professional. Want help finding a number?» |
| **Scope creep** | ≤120 lines. Not «gerontology textbook». Only coaching adaptations |
