## Key Findings & Practical Implications

### 1. Goal-Setting Theory (Locke & Latham)
- Specific, difficult goals consistently outperform "do your best"
- Effect is moderated by task complexity (simpler = stronger)
- Group goals: specific difficult vs nonspecific d = 0.80
- Requires: commitment, feedback, adequate ability

### 2. Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer & Sheeran)
- 94 studies, d = 0.65 — STRONGEST evidence
- If-then planning automates behavior initiation
- Works even under stress, willpower depletion
- Clinical populations: d = 1.10 (very large)

### 3. WOOP / Mental Contrasting (Oettingen)
- Pure positive fantasy PREDICTS WORSE outcomes
- Mental contrasting corrects by adding obstacle awareness
- Interactive delivery stronger (g = 0.465) than document (g = 0.277)
- Cultural boundary: less effective in collectivist cultures
- Backfires when expectations very low (but this is adaptive disengagement)

### 4. Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan)
- Three universal needs: Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness
- Intrinsic goals > extrinsic for well-being
- Self-concordant goals (aligned with values) predict attainment + well-being
- Supporting autonomy increases motivation and persistence

### 5. Habit Formation (Lally, Wood, Neal, Fogg, Clear)
- Median: 66 days to form habit (range: 18-254)
- Context-dependent repetition is key
- Habit stacking: attach new to existing
- Implementation intentions = habit formation accelerator

### 6. Wheel of Life
- NO psychometric validation (face validity only)
- Strong as coaching engagement tool
- Use for conversation facilitation, not measurement

### 7. Values Clarification (Schwartz PVQ)
- HIGH validation: 100+ studies, 50+ countries
- PVQ-21 recommended (best balance validity/brevity)
- Circumplex structure confirmed cross-culturally
- Measurement invariance: CFI >0.92

### 8. Weekly Reviews / Reflection
- 15 min reflection = 23% performance improvement
- Closes open loops (Zeigarnik effect)
- Progress monitoring increases goal attainment
- Small wins boost motivation (Progress Principle)

## Debunked Myths

| Myth | Truth | Source |
|------|-------|--------|
| "21 days to form a habit" | Median 66 days, range 18-254 | Lally et al. (2010) |
| "Positive thinking helps" | Pure positive fantasy HURTS | Oettingen & Mayer (2002) |
| "Grit is key to success" | Grit ≈ conscientiousness (r=.84) | Credé et al. meta-analysis |
| "Willpower is like a muscle" | Ego depletion theory contested | Hagger et al. (2016) failed replication |
